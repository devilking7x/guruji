# 🎬 Guruji — Demo Video Script (2–3 minute)

> Roman Hindi narration. Har shot ~10-15 second. Total ~2:45.

---

## Shot 1 — Hook (0:00–0:15)
**Screen:** Guruji home (violet theme, chat tab).
**Bolo:** "Crores of bacche Hindi me padhte hain, par AI tutors English me jawab dete hain. Meet **Guruji** — Classes 6 se 10 tak ka Hindi-first AI tutor, jo har bacche ko **yaad** rakhta hai."

## Shot 2 — Socratic chat (0:15–0:35)
**Screen:** Chat me type karo: *"Photosynthesis kya hota hai?"*
**Bolo:** "Sawal poochho Hindi ya English me. Aur dekho — Guruji seedha answer nahi deta. Wo **hint deta hai, tumse sochwata hai** — jaise ek achha teacher. Jawab NCERT chapter citation ke saath aata hai."
**Dikhao:** Chapter citation chip + hint-ladder follow-up.

## Shot 3 — Copy check 📸 (0:35–0:55) ✨ Naya
**Screen:** Copy check tab → notebook photo upload → feedback.
**Bolo:** "Ye hai **Copy check**. Baccha apni notebook ka photo le — Guruji pehli galat step pakadta hai aur ek guiding question poochhta hai. Full solution kabhi nahi — aur photo **save bhi nahi hoti**, privacy first."

## Shot 4 — Quiz + XP 🏆 (0:55–1:15)
**Screen:** Quiz tab → "Prakash" topic → 5 sawal → submit → XP/streak/badges.
**Bolo:** "Adaptive quiz — tumhare level ke hisaab se mushkil. Galat sawal apne-aap **revision cards** ban jaate hain. Sahi jawab par XP, streaks aur badges."

## Shot 5 — Revision + Planner 🗓 (1:15–1:35) ✨ Naya
**Screen:** Revision tab (flip cards) → Planner tab (day-wise checklist).
**Bolo:** "Smart revision — bhoolne se pehle yaad dilaye. Aur **Study planner**: chapters, din aur roz ke minutes batao — poora day-wise plan taiyaar, revision ke saath."

## Shot 6 — Worksheet + Voice 🖨🎙 (1:35–1:50) ✨ Naya
**Screen:** Worksheet tab → print preview; phir chat me mic dabao.
**Bolo:** "Kamzor topics se **printable worksheets** — ek click me print. Aur poora app **bol kar** bhi chalao — Hindi voice me sawal, Hindi me jawab."

## Shot 7 — Teacher + Parent 👩‍🏫 (1:50–2:15) ✨ Naya
**Screen:** Teacher tab (class 8 aggregates) → Mata-Pita tab.
**Bolo:** "Teachers ke liye **anonymous dashboard** — poori class ke kamzor topics, bina kisi bacche ka naam jaane. Parents ke liye weekly report. Privacy har jagah: nicknames, aggregates, zero personal data."

## Shot 8 — Challenge + Leaderboard (2:15–2:30) ✨ Naya
**Screen:** Challenge tab → aaj ka sawal → leaderboard top-10.
**Bolo:** "Roz ek **daily challenge**, XP bonus — aur anonymous **leaderboard** me apna nickname dekho."

## Shot 9 — Close (2:30–2:45)
**Screen:** Progress tab (mastery bars) → repo link.
**Bolo:** "Guruji — Socratic tutor, 111 NCERT chapters, photo feedback, planner, worksheets, teacher dashboard. **Open source, MIT licensed.** Har bacche ke liye, uski bhasha me. 🙏"

---

**Recording tips:** 1080p, har click se pehle 1-sec pause, narration visuals se aage na bhaage. Backend live ho to real LLM jawab dikhao, warna mock mode me UI flow.
